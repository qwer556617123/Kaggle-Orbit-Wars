# Third-party provenance and license review

The strongest agents in this repository include lines derived from public
Kaggle competition code. This repository is not offered under a repository-wide
open-source license until the provenance and redistribution terms of each
upstream source have been reviewed.

Before reusing or redistributing an agent, identify its upstream source in
`docs/experiments.md`, preserve any required notices, and comply with that
source's license and the Kaggle competition terms.

Original documentation and evaluation tooling may be separately licensable
after this review is complete.
